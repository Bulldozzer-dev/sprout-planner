Scheduling decisions & boundaries

Overlapping availability is merged. Fixed classes are subtracted. Tasks are handled by priority, then earliest deadline, then input order, and placed in the earliest continuous gap. An optional deadline is the end of that day. No task is split, and breaks must be reserved in your inputs. This greedy heuristic does not guarantee an optimal schedule.

Calendar times are floating local times, so your calendar uses its own timezone. No account or cloud sync is provided. Saving reports storage failures; downloaded backups are the recovery path.
