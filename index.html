How it works & limitations

The planner merges overlapping availability, subtracts fixed classes, then places whole tasks in the earliest gap that fits. Priority 1 is handled first; equal priorities keep their input order. This is a greedy scheduling heuristic, not AI or a guarantee of the best possible timetable. It plans one repeating week, with no deadlines, breaks, accounts or persistent storage. Unscheduled tasks need a larger window or a shorter duration.

Built with AI assistance as a portfolio prototype. No analytics, server or external API calls. Inputs stay on this page and are cleared on reload.
